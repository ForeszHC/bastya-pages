Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About the application

Bástya Smart Home is a personal, self-hosted smart home dashboard.

Google Calendar data

The application requests access to Google Calendar data only after authorization by the Google account owner. Calendar information is used to display calendar events within the dashboard.

Data storage and sharing

Calendar data and authentication credentials are processed by the self-hosted application. Google Calendar data is not sold or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Users can revoke Google account access at Google Account Connections.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application administrator using the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
